# Jiseek AI Transformation — Direction Forward

## Positioning

**Jiseek AI Transformation** helps organisations move from AI experiments to measurable work transformation.

The practice focuses on business outcomes first: identifying where AI can create value, redesigning workflows, implementing practical solutions, building organisational capability, and helping leaders scale responsibly.

## Brand architecture

- **Company:** Jiseek
- **Commercial practice:** Jiseek AI Transformation
- **Principal:** Jim Wong, Founder & Principal, AI Transformation
- **Core promise:** From AI experiments to measurable work transformation.
- **Training/capability:** retained as an enabling capability rather than the primary commercial identity

## Jiseek AI Transformation Framework

1. **Discover** — understand priorities, pain points, workflows and readiness.
2. **Prioritise** — assess opportunities by business value, feasibility, readiness, risk and workforce impact.
3. **Prototype** — test the highest-value use cases quickly with real users and real work.
4. **Transform** — redesign workflows, implement solutions and support adoption.
5. **Scale** — build governance, capability and measurement for sustained impact.

## Commercial offer ladder

1. **Executive AI Briefing** — leadership alignment and opportunity framing.
2. **AI Opportunity Diagnostic** — recommended entry point; workflow mapping, use-case portfolio and roadmap.
3. **AI Transformation Sprint** — rapid implementation of selected use cases and workflow redesign.
4. **AI Transformation Programme** — broader multi-workstream transformation.
5. **AI Transformation Advisory** — ongoing senior advisory for priorities, investments, vendors, governance and change.
6. **AI Transformation Academy** — capability building connected directly to transformation priorities.

## Website conversion principle

The website should not sell generic AI training first. It should answer four executive questions:

1. Where can AI create measurable value in our organisation?
2. Which workflows should change?
3. How do we implement safely and pragmatically?
4. How do we build the internal capability to sustain the change?

The primary CTA is therefore **Book an AI Opportunity Diagnostic / Start a Diagnostic Conversation**.

## Existing programmes

Current public and corporate learning programmes remain useful and should be retained. They should be presented as **Capability Programmes** and as part of the broader transformation system, not as the defining identity of Jiseek.
