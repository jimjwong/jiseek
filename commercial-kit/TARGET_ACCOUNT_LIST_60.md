# Jiseek — First 60 Target Accounts

Purpose: create a focused 60-account pursuit list for the first commercial wave of Jiseek AI Transformation.

Scoring logic:
- **Warmth / access** — existing relationship, prior engagement or plausible referral path.
- **Transformation fit** — meaningful workflow, AI, capability or operational problem.
- **Contract potential** — ability to support S$15k–S$150k+ work.
- **Speed** — likelihood of reaching a decision-maker quickly.
- **Multiplier value** — ability to open member companies, sectors or partner channels.

Priority guide:
- **A = contact now / this week**
- **B = develop over 30 days**
- **C = nurture / referral / ecosystem**

# Account scoring fields for CRM

For each account add:
- Relationship strength: 1–5
- Decision-maker access: 1–5
- Pain urgency: 1–5
- Contract potential: 1–5
- Multiplier value: 1–5
- Procurement friction: 1–5 (reverse score)
- Next action
- Next action date
- Estimated first engagement value
- Expansion potential

# Qualification rule

An account moves from target to qualified opportunity only when at least four are true:
1. A senior sponsor acknowledges a real workflow / productivity / transformation problem.
2. There is a plausible budget owner.
3. There is a business process or workforce population large enough to justify intervention.
4. There is willingness to provide stakeholder access or workflow data.
5. A decision or next step can happen within 90 days.

# Core message

Jiseek is not approaching these accounts to sell another AI course.

The opening proposition is:

> **We help organisations move from AI experimentation to measurable work transformation — identifying the right workflows, redesigning how work gets done, implementing practical solutions and building the internal capability to sustain the change.**

Training is offered where it enables the transformation, not as the centre of the proposition.
