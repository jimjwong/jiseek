# Change Summary

This branch repositions Jiseek from a broad education/training/consultancy brand into a focused **AI Transformation** practice while retaining capability programmes as an enabling part of the offer.

## Changed

- Homepage: new executive-level positioning, problem framing, transformation framework, diagnostic offer, service ladder, target client groups and CTA.
- Services: restructured around Executive AI Briefing, AI Opportunity Diagnostic, AI Transformation Sprint, AI Transformation Programme, AI Transformation Advisory and AI Transformation Academy.
- About: repositioned around AI work transformation, measurable value and Jim Wong as Founder & Principal, AI Transformation.
- Contact: converted from generic inquiry/training contact into a diagnostic-focused lead qualification flow.
- Navigation/footer: AI Transformation now leads; training becomes Capability Programmes; primary CTA becomes Book a Diagnostic.
- SEO: updated title and meta description.

## Unchanged intentionally

- Existing logo and core visual identity.
- Existing capability programme catalogue.
- Existing technical stack and deployment configuration.
- Production `main` branch.

## Commercial principle

The new site sells **clarity, workflow redesign, implementation and measurable outcomes** before it sells training. Training remains a valid product, but no longer defines the company.
