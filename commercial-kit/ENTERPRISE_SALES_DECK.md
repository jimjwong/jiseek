## Slide 6 — Jiseek AI Opportunity Map

Every use case is assessed across:

1. **Business Value**
2. **Feasibility**
3. **Readiness**
4. **Risk**
5. **Workforce Impact**

Decision logic:
- High value + feasible → implement / prototype now
- High value + difficult → strategic initiative
- Lower value + easy → selective productivity quick win
- Lower value + difficult → deprioritise

The goal is disciplined allocation of attention, budget and change capacity.

## Slide 9 — Evidence by Design

A successful workshop is not the same thing as a successful transformation.

Where work moves into implementation, Jiseek establishes evidence around:

### Productivity
Time saved, cycle-time reduction, throughput.

### Business outcome
Cost, revenue, service, quality or decision improvements relevant to the use case.

### Adoption
Whether the redesigned workflow is consistently used.

### Readiness & risk
Capability, governance, data and operational constraints affecting scale.

**Measure the change, not the enthusiasm.**
