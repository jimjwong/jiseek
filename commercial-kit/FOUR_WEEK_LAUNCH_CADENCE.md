# Jiseek Four-Week Commercial Launch Cadence

Objective: convert the new positioning into real conversations, qualified pipeline and the first paid AI Transformation engagements.

## Week 1 — Activate the Warm Network

### Commercial actions
- Build first list of 60 target organisations.
- Prioritise first 20 based on relationship strength and AI urgency.
- Send 15–20 personal messages; no mass blast.
- Book 5+ discovery conversations.
- Prepare the Diagnostic one-pager and sales deck for live use.

### Asset actions
- Replace testimonial placeholders when real testimonials are supplied.
- Select three past projects that can become credible case studies.
- Gather any existing evidence: learner evaluations, client feedback, programme records, workflow artefacts, before / after examples and permission constraints.

### Target outcome
- 5 discovery calls
- 2 qualified opportunities
- at least S$40k qualified pipeline

---

## Week 2 — Diagnose and Scope

### Commercial actions
- Run discovery calls using the playbook.
- Bring additional decision-makers into strong opportunities.
- Offer the AI Opportunity Diagnostic where the problem is broad or prioritisation is unclear.
- Send proposals within 1–2 business days of qualified calls.
- Continue 15 targeted outreaches.

### Target outcome
- cumulative 10+ discovery conversations
- 4–5 qualified opportunities
- 2–3 proposals
- S$100k+ qualified pipeline

---

## Week 3 — Close the First Paid Work

### Commercial actions
- Follow up every active proposal with a decision path.
- Do not reduce price reflexively; reduce scope if budget is genuinely constrained.
- Ask strong contacts for one relevant introduction each.
- Start delivery planning immediately for signed diagnostics.
- Continue outbound selectively; prioritise active opportunities.

### Target outcome
- first paid Diagnostic or equivalent engagement signed
- S$20k–50k contracted revenue
- S$150k–250k qualified pipeline

---

## Week 4 — Deliver, Learn, Expand

### Commercial actions
- Start first engagement with measurement built in from day one.
- Capture language clients use to describe the problem; feed it back into sales copy.
- Identify expansion path during delivery: Diagnostic → Sprint → Programme / Advisory / Academy.
- Ask satisfied stakeholders for introductions, testimonials or permissioned case evidence at the appropriate time.
- Review pipeline and remove low-quality opportunities.

### Target outcome
- one active paid engagement
- second engagement progressing toward close
- at least S$250k qualified pipeline
- clear case-study evidence plan for all new implementation work

---

# Weekly Operating Rhythm

## Monday
- pipeline review
- choose 15 priority accounts
- define next action for every qualified deal

## Tuesday–Thursday
- discovery calls
- stakeholder meetings
- proposals
- targeted outreach
- delivery work for active clients

## Friday
- proposal / follow-up sweep
- update CRM
- capture client insights into reusable IP
- review contracted revenue, collected cash and pipeline coverage

# First-Month Scorecard

Aim for:
- 60 target organisations identified
- 30+ personally contacted
- 12–15 discovery conversations
- 6–8 qualified opportunities
- 4–5 proposals
- 1–2 paid Diagnostics
- 1 larger Transformation opportunity in scope / proposal
- S$50k+ contracted revenue

The exact numbers may vary; the commercial principle does not: **sell while refining the assets, and use real conversations to improve the positioning.**
