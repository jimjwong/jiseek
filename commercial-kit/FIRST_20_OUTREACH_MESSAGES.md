# Jiseek — First 20 Outreach Messages

Use these as starting points. Personalise based on the actual relationship and recent context before sending.

Principles:
- Do not lead with “AI training”.
- Do not send the deck in the first message unless asked.
- Ask for a short conversation, not a purchase.
- Keep the first contact focused on a business / workflow problem.
- For warm accounts, reference the existing relationship naturally.
