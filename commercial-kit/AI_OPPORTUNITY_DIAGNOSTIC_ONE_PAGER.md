# Jiseek AI Opportunity Diagnostic

## From AI experimentation to a prioritised transformation roadmap

Many organisations are already experimenting with AI. The harder question is not whether AI can be used, but **where it can materially improve work, what should be implemented first, and how value should be measured**.

The Jiseek AI Opportunity Diagnostic is a focused **2–3 week engagement** designed to answer those questions before an organisation commits to a larger AI programme, platform or implementation.

## What we do

### 1. Leadership & stakeholder discovery
We clarify strategic priorities, current AI activity, pain points, constraints and expectations with the people closest to the work.

### 2. Workflow & readiness assessment
We examine selected workflows, decisions and recurring tasks to understand where friction, manual effort, delays or knowledge bottlenecks exist.

### 3. AI Opportunity Map
Potential use cases are assessed across five dimensions:

**Business Value × Feasibility × Readiness × Risk × Workforce Impact**

This separates attractive opportunities from low-value AI activity.

### 4. Prioritisation & business logic
We identify which opportunities should be implemented now, tested next, treated as strategic projects or deprioritised.

### 5. 90-day transformation roadmap
Leadership receives a practical next-step plan with priorities, owners, measurement logic and recommended engagement path.

## What you receive

- Executive findings and decision brief
- Current-state workflow observations
- AI maturity / readiness findings
- Prioritised AI use-case portfolio
- Jiseek AI Opportunity Map
- Benefit hypotheses and measurement approach
- Risk and workforce considerations
- 90-day priorities and transformation roadmap
- Recommendation on whether to proceed to a Sprint, broader Transformation Programme, Advisory or internal implementation

## Typical scope

- **Duration:** 2–3 weeks
- **Stakeholders:** approximately 5–12 interviews / working sessions depending on scope
- **Focus:** selected business function, department or cross-functional workflow set
- **Commercial guide:** S$15,000–S$25,000 depending on breadth and complexity

## Who it is for

Leadership teams that are asking questions such as:

- Where should we actually use AI?
- Which of our current experiments are worth scaling?
- What workflows should be redesigned rather than merely automated?
- How do we avoid buying another tool without a clear business case?
- How should we measure whether AI is creating value?
- What should we do in the next 90 days?
