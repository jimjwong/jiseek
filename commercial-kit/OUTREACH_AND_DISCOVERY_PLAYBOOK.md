# Jiseek Outreach & Discovery Playbook

Goal: create qualified conversations with leadership teams that have moved beyond AI curiosity and now need clarity on implementation, workflow redesign and measurable value.

---

# 1. Who to Contact First

Prioritise warm and credible relationships before cold outbound.

## Tier 1 — Existing / warm network
- public-sector and infrastructure contacts
- education institutions
- existing corporate training clients
- past employers and transformation contacts
- associations and professional bodies
- Microsoft / technology ecosystem contacts
- SME owners and senior operators already known to Jim

## Buyer roles
- CEO / Managing Director
- COO
- CIO / CTO
- CHRO / Head of People
- Transformation Director
- Innovation / Digital Director
- Business-unit HOD
- Operations Director

## Trigger signals
Good prospects often say:
- “Everyone is already using ChatGPT, but we do not know what comes next.”
- “We have too many AI ideas.”
- “We bought licenses but adoption is inconsistent.”
- “We need use cases for management.”
- “We want productivity gains but cannot quantify them.”
- “We need governance before scaling.”
- “Our teams need more than another generic AI workshop.”

I’m speaking with a few organisations about what is getting difficult as they move from trying AI to actually operationalising it. Would be keen to hear what you’re seeing on your side. Free for a short chat next week?

If anyone in your network is currently wrestling with that — especially a COO, CIO, CHRO, transformation lead or HOD — I’d appreciate an introduction. I’m currently taking on a small number of diagnostic engagements.

# 3. Discovery Call Objective

Do not pitch the entire Jiseek service catalogue.

The objective is to determine:

1. Is there a meaningful business / operating problem?
2. Is AI genuinely relevant?
3. Is there senior sponsorship or decision authority?
4. Is the organisation prepared to invest in diagnosis / implementation?
5. What should the next paid step be?

A successful call ends with either:
- no fit;
- further stakeholder discovery;
- a paid AI Opportunity Diagnostic;
- a directly scoped Sprint / Programme if the problem is already clear.

---

# 4. Discovery Questions

## Context
- What is driving the AI conversation internally right now?
- What has already been tried?
- Which teams are using AI today?
- What has management asked you to achieve?

## Business problem
- Which workflows or areas of work are causing the most friction?
- Where are people spending disproportionate time on manual, repetitive or knowledge-heavy work?
- Where are delays, handoffs, rework or decision bottlenecks occurring?
- Which outcomes matter most: cost, productivity, service, revenue, quality, risk or employee capacity?

## Existing AI activity
- What AI tools or licenses are already in use?
- Which experiments appear promising?
- Which have stalled?
- Is there a central AI strategy or is activity distributed across teams?

## Measurement
- How would leadership know that an AI initiative had been worthwhile?
- Do you currently have baseline time / cost / quality measures for the workflows in question?
- Is management expecting a quantified business case?

## Readiness
- Who would own implementation?
- What data, security or governance constraints matter?
- How open are employees and managers to workflow change?
- Is there internal technical capability to implement solutions once priorities are clear?

## Buying process
- Who else needs to be involved in deciding next steps?
- Is there a budget already allocated for AI / transformation / job redesign / capability building?
- What timing is management working toward?

# 5. Qualification Score

Score each opportunity 0–2 on each dimension.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business pain | vague | useful | material / urgent |
| Senior sponsorship | none | influencer | decision-maker / sponsor |
| AI relevance | weak | possible | clear |
| Workflow access | unavailable | partial | accessible |
| Budget potential | none | uncertain | credible |
| Timing | someday | this year | next 90 days |
| Implementation appetite | curiosity | exploratory | committed |

**11–14:** priority opportunity  
**7–10:** nurture / further qualify  
**0–6:** do not spend heavy selling time

# 6. How to Introduce the Diagnostic

Use the client's problem first.

Example:

“From what you’ve described, I would not recommend starting with another broad AI workshop or choosing a platform yet. The bigger question is which of these workflows are actually worth changing and what the business case looks like. That is what our AI Opportunity Diagnostic is designed to answer.”

Then explain:
- 2–3 weeks
- stakeholder and workflow discovery
- prioritised opportunity portfolio
- value / feasibility / readiness / risk / workforce-impact assessment
- 90-day roadmap
- S$15k–25k depending on scope

Do not apologise for the price. Change scope if necessary rather than immediately discounting.

---

# 7. Closing the Call

## If qualified

“Based on today, I think there is enough here to frame a Diagnostic. I’ll summarise the business problem, proposed scope, stakeholders and outputs in a short proposal. If that reflects what you need, we can start with the discovery phase.”

## If more discovery is required

“The opportunity looks meaningful, but before scoping it I’d like to hear from [COO / process owner / IT / HR / sponsor]. Can we bring them into the next discussion?”

## If not qualified

“I don’t think you need a transformation engagement yet. The more appropriate next step is probably [executive briefing / capability programme / internal experiment].”

This protects positioning and trust.

---

# 8. Follow-Up Rhythm

After discovery:
- same day: short written recap and agreed next step
- within 1–2 business days: proposal if qualified
- 3–4 days after proposal: decision / questions follow-up
- one week later: final active follow-up before moving to nurture

Do not leave proposals open indefinitely without a decision path.

---

# 9. Weekly Commercial Targets

Initial operating cadence:
- 15 targeted personal outreaches
- 5 meaningful conversations
- 2 qualified opportunities
- 1–2 proposals
- maintain approximately 3× pipeline coverage against near-term contracted-revenue target

Measure:
- qualified pipeline value
- discovery calls
- proposal value
- close rate
- average contract value
- contracted revenue
- collected cash
- expansion revenue

Do not optimise primarily for website traffic, followers or content volume.
