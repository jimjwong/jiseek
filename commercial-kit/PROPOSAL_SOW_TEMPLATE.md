## 1. Executive Summary

[Client] is currently exploring how AI can improve [business area / workflows / operating priority]. The opportunity is not simply to introduce more tools, but to identify where AI can create measurable value, redesign the surrounding work and establish a practical path to implementation.

Jiseek proposes a [2–3 week AI Opportunity Diagnostic / 6–8 week Transformation Sprint / broader Transformation Programme] to help [Client] move from experimentation to a prioritised, evidence-led transformation plan.

### Intended outcome
By the end of the engagement, leadership will have a clear view of:
- the highest-value AI opportunities in scope;
- which workflows should be redesigned;
- feasibility, readiness, risk and workforce considerations;
- what to implement or test next;
- how value should be measured; and
- the recommended 90-day execution path.

## 3. Scope of Work

### Phase 1 — Discover
- Kick-off with sponsor / project team
- Leadership and stakeholder interviews
- Review of selected workflows, documents and current AI activity
- Current-state observations and readiness assessment

### Phase 2 — Prioritise
- Identify candidate AI use cases
- Assess Business Value, Feasibility, Readiness, Risk and Workforce Impact
- Build the Jiseek AI Opportunity Map
- Develop benefit hypotheses and measurement logic

### Phase 3 — Recommend
- Prioritise opportunities into implementation categories
- Define 90-day priorities, owners and dependencies
- Executive readout and decision workshop
- Recommend next engagement path where relevant

Adjust phases for Sprint / Programme scopes.

## 5. Measures of Success

Success will be assessed on clarity and decision usefulness rather than the number of AI ideas generated.

Relevant measures may include:
- workflows reviewed and prioritised;
- decision-ready use cases identified;
- clear business-value hypotheses;
- baseline / measurement approach established;
- agreed owners and next actions;
- leadership alignment on priorities;
- implementation readiness.

For implementation engagements, add use-case-specific productivity, business, quality and adoption metrics.

# Proposal Discipline

Before sending any proposal:
- restate the client's problem using their language;
- lead with business outcome, not AI features;
- include a clear scope boundary;
- use a price appropriate to value and stakeholder complexity;
- avoid discounting before changing scope;
- make the payment trigger explicit;
- include only evidence that can be substantiated;
- end with one clear action to commence.
